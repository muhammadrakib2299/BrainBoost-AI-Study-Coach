BrainBoost — AI Study Coach

Upload any notes, PDF, or textbook chapter. The AI extracts key concepts, generates flashcards and quizzes, tracks what you know, and builds a smart daily study schedule using spaced repetition — so you study smarter, not harder.

Core features

PDF / note upload

Upload PDFs, paste text, or link a URL. AI parses and extracts the key concepts automatically.

AI flashcard generator

Automatically creates Q&A flashcards from your content. Edit or regenerate any card.

Smart quiz mode

Multiple choice, true/false, and fill-in-the-blank quizzes with instant AI explanations.

Spaced repetition

SM-2 algorithm schedules reviews based on how well you know each card. Hard cards come back sooner.

Study schedule

Set your exam date. AI builds a day-by-day study plan prioritizing weak topics first.

Progress dashboard

Track mastery per topic, daily streaks, quiz scores, and time studied over time.

AI explain mode

Got an answer wrong? Ask the AI to explain it in simpler terms or with an example.

Summary generator

One-click summary of uploaded content at different detail levels — brief, medium, or deep.

MVP build roadmap

1

Week 1 — Upload + parse

File upload UI, PDF text extraction, store raw content in DB, basic auth with Supabase.

2

Week 2 — AI flashcard generation

Claude API prompt to extract concepts and generate Q&A pairs. Display, edit, and save flashcard deck.

3

Week 3 — Quiz mode + scoring

Multiple choice quiz from flashcards, score tracking, AI explanation on wrong answers.

4

Week 4 — Spaced repetition engine

Implement SM-2 algorithm, "Easy / Hard / Again" rating per card, next-review scheduling.

5

Week 5 — Dashboard + schedule

Progress charts, topic mastery %, exam date input, AI-generated daily study plan.

6

Week 6 — Polish + launch

Stripe payments, email reminders, mobile responsiveness, deploy to Vercel.
